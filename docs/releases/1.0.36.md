- Unified attachments: on Mac, “+” is now a single “Add Attachments…”; on iPhone/iPad there are “Photos & Videos…” and “Choose File…”. Picking, dragging and pasting all behave the same, and files dropped into the input no longer turn into paths.
- Send videos for the AI to analyze: up to 48 timestamped frames are extracted on your device and sent with the message; the original video and audio are not uploaded. Videos up to 3 minutes and 500 MB are supported.
- Fixed occasional out-of-order uploads or errors when sending many images in a row.
- New “Bark notifications”: configure a Bark push URL per host in Settings to get session-finished and scheduled-task results on your phone; tapping a notification opens the task history. Requires the latest live-activity plugin on the host.
- Scheduled tasks can now turn on “Notify task results” individually.
- A powered-off machine no longer shows “Reconnecting” forever: after 3 failures it shows “Offline”, retries slow down, and after about a day offline automatic connection pauses. You can pause, resume or reconnect at any time; time your computer is asleep or the app is closed doesn't count toward the offline period.
